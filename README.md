📊 LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy - B2B SaaS Research Project

## 🎯 Objective
This project analyzes how top LinkedIn creators in the B2B SaaS space use organic content to build audience, generate demand, and drive business growth.

The goal is to extract real-world patterns from practitioners and convert them into structured insights.

---

## 🧠 Work Completed

- Selected LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy as the research topic
- Identified and studied high-signal LinkedIn SaaS creators
- Collected real LinkedIn posts from multiple experts
- Created structured GitHub repository for research organization
- Organized raw content into categorized folders for analysis
- Created `sources.md` to document selected experts and reasoning

## ⚙️ Data Collection Method
Data was collected manually from LinkedIn by identifying relevant SaaS creators and extracting their public posts.

Selection criteria included:
- Relevance to B2B SaaS content strategy
- Practical applicability of insights
- Consistency in posting and engagement
- Focus on organic growth and demand generation

---

## 🧩 Key Areas of Analysis
This research focuses on identifying patterns in:

- Content hooks and storytelling techniques
- Authority building through personal branding
- Distribution and engagement strategies
- SaaS demand generation via LinkedIn
- Content consistency and algorithm behavior

---

## 📊 Output of This Research
The final output of this project is a structured understanding of:
- How LinkedIn drives SaaS audience growth
- What content formats perform best
- How creators convert content into business outcomes
- Repeatable frameworks for organic LinkedIn growth
